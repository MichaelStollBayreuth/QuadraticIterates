\chapter*{About this blueprint}

This blueprint develops a criterion for the surjectivity of the arboreal Galois representation
attached to $x^2 + a$, $a \in \QQ$, which works directly with the orbit $(c_n)$ of $0$ and with
quadratic characters of $\QQ^\times$, and links each statement to its formalization in Lean~4.
The known results of \cite{paper, li2020, li2021} for integer parameters are derived from the
general statements for rational parameters (Chapter~\ref{chap:integers}).

\emph{Status.} Every definition and theorem is formalized. The aim is not to reproduce the
proofs of \cite{paper, li2020, li2021}, which go through the Möbius quotients $b_n$ of the $c_n$,
but to give different, simpler proofs of their main results and to extend them to further cases
and to rational parameters. Lean names are given in \lname{typewriter} inside the statement or a
\emph{Formalization} remark; Section~\ref{sec:formalization} describes the formalization.

\emph{Standing notation.} $a \in \QQ$, $f = X^2 + a$, $f_0 = X$ and $f_{n+1} = f_n^2 + a$ are
the iterates of $f$, $K_n \subseteq \Qbar$ is the splitting field of $f_n$ over $\QQ$, and
$\Om_n = \Gal(K_n/\QQ)$. $\Ct{n}$ denotes the $n$-fold iterated wreath product of $C_2$ with
itself. From Chapter~\ref{chap:sequence} on, $a = r/s$ with coprime integers $r$, $s$ and
$s \ge 1$.
